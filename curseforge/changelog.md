# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-29)

First version, for Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47 and Cold Sweat 2.4.3.2 or newer.

- Frees about 150 MB of memory. Cold Sweat kept eleven codec debug names of about 14 MB each for its entity requirements. Those codecs now get a short name.
- Logs one line at startup that says whether the fix applied.
- Works on the client, the server or both, and the two don't need to match.
